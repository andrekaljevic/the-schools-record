1. Privacy

Privacy

A restrained data practice

We collect only what is needed to operate the public record and respond to enquiries or corrections.

Last reviewed: 2 September 2026. This page describes the product’s implemented behaviour and is not bespoke legal advice.

Ordinary browsing

The public site does not require an account. It does not use advertising trackers and does not set a profiling cookie. Essential infrastructure may process an IP address and request metadata transiently for security and delivery.

Privacy-respecting analytics

We record a short allow-list of product events, the page path, a coarse lead source and timestamp. We do not place names, email addresses, school selections entered into an enquiry, or free-text form content in analytics.

Enquiries and corrections

If you submit a form, we store the fields you provide so the request can be reviewed and answered. These records are restricted to the fulfilment workflow. The anti-spam field and rate limit are used only to protect the service.

Retention

Unsuccessful spam submissions are not deliberately retained. Genuine enquiries and corrections are reviewed at least annually and deleted when no longer needed for the request, contractual record, editorial audit or a legal obligation. The operational target is 24 months for an inactive commercial enquiry and seven years for an accepted correction decision.

Source privacy

Private document locations, direct source URLs, archived URLs, Drive identifiers, unpublished source files and internal research notes are excluded from browser-accessible output. Stable public source references may be shown instead.

Your choices

You may ask for access to, correction of or deletion of personal information submitted through a form, subject to legitimate editorial and legal record-keeping needs. Use the relevant form and state “privacy request” in the intended-use field.
