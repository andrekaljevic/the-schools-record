Material public-product releases and any future separately approved statistical revisions are recorded here.

  1. 2 September 2026

    Statistical revision v2

    Post-remark examination results control

    Five locked examination-result corrections are reversed on the record owner’s instruction: the earlier published figures are the post-remark results, and the values that had replaced them were recomputed from pre-remark tables. Winchester College GCSE 2016 (69.1 / 94.1) and 2019 (44.4 / 72.4 / 87.5 / 95.3), Winchester Pre-U 2012 D1–D3 (79), St Paul’s School GCSE 2012 top-3 (98.8) and A-level 2014 A* (47.8). Each reversal is a new ledger entry (R-C01, R-C02, R-C03, R-C05, R-C06); the superseded entries remain visible. Corrections concerning university applications, offers and destinations are unchanged.

    Data baseline: 818c7c296fed + revision v2 · snapshot production-818c7c2-data-revised-v2 · statistical change, six values in five rows.

  2. 2 September 2026

    Native parity restoration

    The complete record, natively

    Restored everything the final public product could do that the native rebuild had lost: an evidence centre that searches all 2,277 records and traces any displayed figure to its public sources; Oxford and Cambridge and US-university records for every school and corpus-wide; school-entry evidence; the corrections and published-differences ledgers; ledgers that show every published denominator; approved public source links; honest form receipts; page titles and shareable metadata.

    Data baseline: 818c7c296fed · no statistical change.

  3. 2 September 2026

    Charts

    Results by year

    Added a like-for-like series panel to every row of the school index on one shared ruler, a results-by-year section to each school record with one panel per grading ruler, and the comparison chart to the sample dossier. Lines join consecutive published years only; 2020–21 and the 2010 introduction of A* stay marked.

    Data baseline: 818c7c296fed · no statistical change.
